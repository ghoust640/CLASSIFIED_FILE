SECTION I — GENERATION ONE: THE 1998 "ARCLIGHT" CRISIS

In the late 1990s, Vesperia was undergoing a fragile recovery period following the conclusion of a brutal border war. The original Task Force G7 was commissioned as an elite counter-terrorism strike element, achieving legendary status within underground intelligence networks.

In 1998, a rogue military faction captured a major hydroelectric installation, issuing direct threats to breach the facility and flood an adjacent civilian valley. Citing delicate international optics and diplomatic protocol, the Vesperian administration commanded G7 to stand down and leave negotiations to state officials.

Anticipating absolute diplomatic failure and mass civilian casualties, the original G7 operatives severed communication links, went rogue, and assaulted the facility. The tactical objective was successfully accomplished, but the government immediately disavowed the unit. To bury political fallout, active members were officially branded as traitors and systematically hunted down. All historical records of the unit were permanently expunged from military history.

SECTION II — GENERATION TWO: THE 2020s RESURRECTION

For over two decades, "Group 7" remained a taboo military legend — a cautionary ghost story circulated among cadets to deter insubordination. The destabilization of Nova Kaelen by the Shattered Crown Initiative in the 2020s proved that traditional military doctrines were entirely ineffective.

High command required absolute deniability — operators authorized to execute extreme measures and absorb political blowback. They required ghosts. The military reinstated the G7 framework, sweeping the armed forces for seven isolated outcasts: highly lethal combatants with no family, zero political connections, and nothing left to lose.

Upon squad finalization, operators were briefed on the suppressed truth of Generation One's fate. Rather than retreating, the new team claimed the G7 callsign as a badge of honor. The squad operates with clear-eyed awareness that the Vesperian government may ultimately target them just like their predecessors — a shared acknowledgment of complete expendability that forged an impenetrable psychological bond. They do not fight to serve politicians; their allegiance is bound strictly to Vesperia and to one another.

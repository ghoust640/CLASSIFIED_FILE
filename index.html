Nation State Profile — The United Republic of Vesperia (URV)

Functions as a highly strategic, rapidly expanding economic power, maintaining critical oversight of global shipping lanes and high-capacity deep-water ports. Geographically, the nation is defined by severe topographical and socioeconomic contrast — hyper-wealthy, automated urban hubs walled off from dense, rugged mountain ranges and primordial frontier forests.

Capital Metropolis — Nova Kaelen

Operates as the sprawling national epicenter. Vertical neon-and-glass corporate towers loom directly over decaying, brutalist concrete sectors. The metropolis remains a permanent crucible of high-end industrial espionage, syndicate-run corruption, and violent underground dissent.

Active Threat Matrix — The 2020s Crisis

Vesperia is currently embroiled in an unacknowledged, asymmetric shadow war against the Shattered Crown Initiative (SCI) — a heavily capitalized, ultra-nationalist separatist insurgent faction.

  • Hostile Tactics: The SCI employs modern hybrid warfare, including weaponized commercial drone swarms, infrastructure-wide cyber-disruption, and ruthless urban guerrilla tactics.
  • Operational Deadlock: By intentionally embedding themselves within the high-density civilian populace of Nova Kaelen's slums, the insurgents render traditional military deployment, heavy artillery, and conventional airstrikes entirely unviable. With public panic rising, state command structures are facing structural collapse.

CLASSIFIED MILITARY ARCHIVE // EYES ONLY SUBJECT: TASK FORCE G7 HISTORICAL LINEAGE & DOSSIER EXCERPT
SECTION I — GENERATION ONE: THE 1998 "ARCLIGHT" CRISIS
  • Historical Baseline: In the late 1990s, Vesperia was undergoing a fragile recovery period following the conclusion of a brutal border war.
  • Unit Origin: The original Task Force G7 was commissioned as an elite counter-terrorism strike element, achieving legendary status within underground intelligence networks.
  • The "Arclight" Threat: In 1998, a rogue military faction captured a major hydroelectric installation, issuing direct threats to breach the facility and flood an adjacent civilian valley.
  • Command Interference: Citing delicate international optics and diplomatic protocol, the Vesperian administration commanded G7 to stand down and leave negotiations to state officials.
  • The Rogue Protocol: Anticipating absolute diplomatic failure and mass civilian casualties, the original G7 operatives severed communication links, went rogue, and assaulted the facility.
  • Disavowal and Erasure: The tactical objective was successfully accomplished, but the government immediately disavowed the unit. To bury political fallout, active members were officially branded as traitors and systematically hunted down. All historical records of the unit were permanently expunged from military history.
SECTION II — GENERATION TWO: THE 2020s RESURRECTION
  • Post-1998 Status: For over two decades, "Group 7" remained a taboo military legend — a cautionary ghost story circulated among cadets to deter insubordination.
  • Operational Crisis: The destabilization of Nova Kaelen by the Shattered Crown Initiative (SCI) in the 2020s proved that traditional military doctrines were entirely ineffective.
  • Tactical Need: High command required absolute deniability — operators authorized to execute extreme measures and absorb political blowback. They required ghosts.
  • Recruitment Parameters: The military reinstated the G7 framework, sweeping the armed forces for seven isolated outcasts: highly lethal combatants with no family, zero political connections, and nothing left to lose.
  • Indoctrination: Upon squad finalization, operators were briefed on the suppressed truth of Generation One's fate. Rather than retreating, the new team claimed the G7 callsign as a badge of honor.
  • Psychological Profile & Bonding: The squad operates with clear-eyed awareness that the Vesperian government may ultimately target them just like their predecessors. This shared acknowledgment of complete expendability forged an impenetrable psychological bond. They do not fight to serve politicians; their allegiance is bound strictly to Vesperia and to one another.
